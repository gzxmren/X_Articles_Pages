01 · WHY INSIDER FLOW IS THE ONLY EDGE LEFT FOR RETAIL

The unfair game
You are not going to out-trade the market on latency. Not on capital. Not on info-flow. There is a warehouse of GPUs in Frankfurt reading the mempool faster than your ping to Google.
You are also not going to out-analyze a bonding curve, a launch, or an unlock. Someone with a Bloomberg terminal and three PhDs got there first.
But you can watch the wallets that already have that edge. That is a completely different game.
Every airdrop, every token unlock, every KOL bag, every "coincidentally" early buy of the next narrative - someone knew. That "knew" leaves a fingerprint on-chain. The fingerprint is public. It is just very, very noisy.
Why manual copy-trading fails
A single trader cannot watch 200 wallets across 4 chains around the clock. Not because they lack skill. Because the useful signal is scattered across:
  • A wallet explorer per chain
  • A DEX aggregator per chain
  • A Twitter feed per KOL
  • A Telegram alpha group that leaks in 3 different languages
By the time you've opened all those tabs, whoever built a bot is already sitting on the fill.
Why scripts fail too
The obvious response is "build a bot." That works for a week, then it stops.
Scripts don't know a wallet only prints on weekends. Scripts don't read the token's Twitter and know the meme died three days ago. Scripts don't stand down when SOL dumps 8% in an hour. Scripts don't catch an orchestrated honey pot dressed up as a real insider entry.
The right shape
The right shape is a desk. Five specialists, each doing one job. One that watches. One that judges the wallet. One that judges the token. One that judges the market. One that argues against every trade before capital moves.
Not one bot with five features. Five bots with one focus each.
The distinction is the difference between a script that works for a week and a desk that compounds for three months.

03 · THE REAL EDGE - COPYING THE WALLETS THAT ALREADY WIN

The reframe
I am not smarter than the market. I don't need to be. I need to identify the wallets that already are, and be on their side of every trade within 5 seconds.
Wallet-copying is the only asymmetric bet left for a retail-sized operator. You skip the analysis. You outsource it to the people who were paid to do it, or the people whose position size proves they've done it well.
The desk tracks 187 wallets. They fall into three categories, and each category needs a completely different filter. Where the wallet list comes from
Two DEX/tracker stacks feed the pipeline:
AXIOM
and
GMGN
. Both give real-time wallet activity, PnL leaderboards for the last 24h / 7d / 30d, and one-click "copy wallet" export. AXIOM is faster on execution, GMGN is deeper on historical wallet analytics the desk uses both in parallel.
Every 4 hours, SCOUT pulls the top 200 wallets by realized PnL from each platform, deduplicates, and pushes anything new to HISTORIAN for a fitness audit. Wallets that pass the audit and hold winrate above 55% over the last 14 days get added to the live copy list. Wallets that fall below 40% winrate for 7 days get dropped without ceremony.
The 187 number is not a target. It's the equilibrium the pipeline settles at when the filters are tuned. Loosen them and it drifts to 300+. Tighten and it drops to 60. Neither prints better than 187.
Class 1 · OG operators (~30 wallets)
Active since 2019 or earlier. Multi-cycle survivors. Slow to enter, slow to exit. Almost never wrong on a new narrative, but if you follow them into a token, you're often 6 hours late - that's fine, because they hold for weeks.
Copy weight: high. Hold time: long.
Class 2 · Sniper wallets (~90 wallets)
Get filled on new launches in the first block. Usually connected to launch teams, MEV crews, or infrastructure providers. Sharpest signals on the desk. Also the most dangerous - 1 in 12 of their entries is a honey pot they seeded to bait copy-traders.
Copy weight: medium, gated by DEVIL (see below). Hold time: minutes to hours.
Class 3 · KOL bags (~65 wallets)
Wallets tied to Twitter accounts with >50K followers. They call trades publicly after they're in. Loudest signals. Least reliable. Great when the market is hot, disastrous in a chop.
Copy weight: low, only activated when PULSE says the market permits it. Hold time: short.

04 · THE FIVE AGENTS

Each agent runs as an independent Python process on a persistent cloud VM. They share one Redis instance for state and one Postgres table for the trade log. If any single agent crashes, the others keep running and the coordinator downgrades confidence until it recovers.
Agent 1 · SCOUT
The only agent that doesn't use an LLM. Pure code. Job: watch 187 wallets over WebSockets and emit a signal every time one opens a position.
class Scout:
    """Multi-chain WebSocket wallet watcher. No LLM. Fires signals on entry."""

    def __init__(self, wallets: list[Wallet], rpc_endpoints: dict):
        self.wallets_by_chain = self._group_by_chain(wallets)
        self.rpc = rpc_endpoints
        self.signals = asyncio.Queue()

    async def run(self):
        await asyncio.gather(*[
            self._watch_chain(chain, wallets)
            for chain, wallets in self.wallets_by_chain.items()
        ])

    async def _watch_chain(self, chain: str, wallets: set[str]):
        async with websockets.connect(self.rpc[chain]) as ws:
            await ws.send(self._subscribe_payload(chain, wallets))
            async for msg in ws:
                tx = self._parse_tx(chain, json.loads(msg))
                if tx is None or tx.side != "buy":
                    continue
                if tx.wallet not in wallets:
                    continue
                await self.signals.put(InsiderSignal(
                    wallet=tx.wallet,
                    chain=chain,
                    token=tx.token_address,
                    size_usd=tx.notional_usd,
                    wallet_class=self._class_of(tx.wallet),
                    ts=tx.block_time,
                ))
Production latency: block confirmation to signal-in-queue in under 500ms across all four chains. Anything slower and the copy fills at 4% worse than the insider.
Agent 2 · HISTORIAN
First LLM in the chain. One question: does this wallet's history support copying this specific entry?
Some wallets are great on new launches but disaster on anything that graduated to a DEX. Some print only between 22:00-04:00 UTC. Some only work when they're buying alone - the second another insider joins, the whole pattern breaks.
class Historian:
    """LLM-based wallet-fit judge. Reads wallet history against current signal."""

    async def judge(self, signal: InsiderSignal, history: list[Trade]) -> dict:
        hist_str = "\n".join(
            f"  {t.ts} | {t.chain} | {t.token[:8]}.. | {t.side} "
            f"${t.size_usd:>7.0f} | held {t.hold_hours}h | pnl {t.pnl_pct:+.0f}%"
            for t in history[-40:]
        )
        prompt = f"""You audit an insider wallet's fitness for the CURRENT signal.

Wallet: {signal.wallet[:8]}.. (class: {signal.wallet_class})
Current entry: {signal.chain} | {signal.token[:8]}.. | ${signal.size_usd:.0f}

Last 40 trades:
{hist_str}

Rate each 0.0 to 1.0:
1. history_fit: does this entry match the wallet's WINNING pattern (not just any pattern)?
2. recent_form: last 10 trades - trending winners or losers?
3. size_anomaly: is this entry unusually small or large for this wallet? (0 = normal, 1 = anomaly)
4. copy_score: overall recommendation.

Reply ONLY JSON:
{{"history_fit": 0.0, "recent_form": 0.0, "size_anomaly": 0.0, "copy_score": 0.0}}"""
        return await self.grok_call(prompt, model="grok-4-fast")
This is exactly where a metrics-only script breaks. A pure numbers approach cannot tell that a wallet's last five trades were all in the same DeFi sub-sector and this sixth one departs from the pattern for a reason.
Below copy_score of 0.4, the desk drops the signal. No further agent calls. Saves ~$0.08 per veto and gets us back to watching.
Agent 3 · CONTEXT
The narrative agent. Job: is what they're buying actually alive?
Reads token metadata, holders, socials, DEX volume trajectory, and - where relevant - the current live crypto Twitter sentiment on the narrative the token belongs to.
class Context:
    """LLM-based token/narrative scorer."""

    async def score(self, token: TokenSnapshot, signal: InsiderSignal) -> dict:
        prompt = f"""You evaluate the live health of a token an insider just entered.

Token: {token.name} ({token.symbol}) on {token.chain}
Age: {token.age_hours}h | Holders: {token.holders} | Vol 1h: ${token.vol_1h_usd:.0f}
Socials: twitter @{token.twitter or 'none'}, tg {token.tg_members or 0} members
Narrative it belongs to: {token.narrative or 'unclassified'}
Insider entered at market cap: ${signal.token_mc_usd:.0f}

Rate each 0.0 to 1.0:
1. token_health: are holders growing or fleeing right now?
2. narrative_life: is the narrative gaining attention today, or dying?
3. execution_room: can we get filled at reasonable slippage at our size?
4. red_flags: any concentration risk, mint authority, blacklist function?

Reply ONLY JSON:
{{"token_health": 0.0, "narrative_life": 0.0, "execution_room": 0.0, "red_flags": 0.0}}"""
        return await self.grok_call(prompt, model="grok-4-fast")
CONTEXT is the only agent that can veto a copy_score of 0.9 from HISTORIAN. If an insider you trust just bought a token with a dying meme and no volume, you skip. Insiders are wrong too. This is where you catch it.
Agent 4 · PULSE
The macro-mood agent. Does the whole market permit new entries right now?
Runs on a 10-minute cache. If BTC drops 4% in an hour, or funding flips sharply negative on perps, or a major exchange goes into maintenance - PULSE goes cold and the whole desk stands down regardless of anything else.
class Pulse:
    """Market-mood assessor. 10-minute cache."""

    async def read(self) -> dict:
        if self._fresh():
            return self._cache
        ctx = await self._fetch_market()
        prompt = f"""You judge whether NOW is a good moment to open new copy positions.

BTC 24h: {ctx['btc_24h']}% | 1h: {ctx['btc_1h']}%
ETH/BTC ratio 24h: {ctx['ethbtc_24h']}%
Total DEX vol delta 4h: {ctx['dex_vol_delta']}%
Funding avg (top 10 perps): {ctx['funding_avg']:.3f}%
Fear/Greed: {ctx['fng']} | Hour UTC: {ctx['hour_utc']}

Rate each 0.0 to 1.0:
1. mood: panic vs greed
2. rotation: is capital flowing INTO risk-on or OUT of it
3. liquidity: is on-chain liquidity healthy or drying
4. go_signal: overall entry permission. 0.0 = stand down, 1.0 = full send.

Reply ONLY JSON:
{{"mood": 0.0, "rotation": 0.0, "liquidity": 0.0, "go_signal": 0.0}}"""
        self._cache = await self.grok_call(prompt, model="grok-4-fast")
        self._cache_ts = time.time()
        return self._cache
A go_signal below 0.3 halts new entries desk-wide. Exit rules keep running. This one line of code caught the worst of two double-digit drawdown days in month two.
Agent 5 · DEVIL
The adversarial one. Runs on the stronger model. The other four work toward approval. DEVIL works toward veto.
Its only job: find a reason NOT to copy this signal, given everything the others said.
class Devil:
    """Adversarial pre-execution veto. Stronger model. Argues against every trade."""

    async def check(self, signal, history_res, context_res, pulse_res, composite) -> dict:
        prompt = f"""You are the last check before capital moves. Every other agent
has APPROVED. Your job is to find a reason NOT to copy this signal.

Signal: {signal.wallet_class} wallet {signal.wallet[:8]}.. bought ${signal.size_usd:.0f}
of {signal.token[:8]}.. on {signal.chain}. Composite: {composite:.2f}

Historian: {json.dumps(history_res)}
Context: {json.dumps(context_res)}
Pulse: {json.dumps(pulse_res)}

Look for:
- Contradictions between agents (e.g. narrative dying but PULSE greedy)
- Hidden risk (concentration, timing, wallet class × narrative mismatch)
- Position size anomaly
- Whether this looks like a honey pot the insider seeded

Reply ONLY JSON:
{{"approve": true, "confidence": 0.0, "veto_reason": "", "risk_flags": []}}

approve=false on any serious concern. Missing 10 good trades is cheaper than
taking 1 honey pot. Be adversarial by default."""
        return await self.grok_call(prompt, model="grok-4")

06 · WHAT DID NOT WORK

The desk did not print $1M by shipping v1. Here's what broke, in order.
Days 1-4 · Naive copy of everything
Copied all 187 wallets, no filters, no classes. Down 63% in the first 96 hours.
Insiders are wrong ~45% of the time. Un-filtered copying inherits every one of their bad trades with lag and slippage on top. This is exactly why HISTORIAN exists.
Days 5-9 · Filters too tight
Overcorrected. Set composite threshold at 0.80. In four days: 8 trades, no losses, no gains, and a rental bill.
Dropped threshold to 0.62 and watched what happened. Trade rate went from 2/day to 34/day. Win rate stayed above 65%. Now it earned.
Days 10-15 · No macro layer
Made $28K Monday-Wednesday. Gave back $41K Sunday night when BTC flushed and every alt followed.
Added PULSE the next day. Since then, daily variance is down roughly 45% and drawdown days rarely exceed the previous week's gains.
Day 16 · No adversarial check
Composite score of 0.74 fired on a token with a real sniper wallet entry, healthy narrative, and permissive market. Turned out to be an orchestrated honey pot seeded by the sniper's own crew. Lost $12,600 on it in 90 seconds.
Added DEVIL the next day. It has since flagged 3 near-identical setups. Two would have been the same trap.
Day 30-91 · Compounding
Every failure above is a permanent piece of the pipeline. Once nothing new was breaking, the desk started actually earning. The last 30 days account for ~$680K of the $1M total.
That is the whole point of the architecture. Not every idea working. All the failed ideas caught early enough that the working one has room.

07 · THE SIX-HOUR BUG THAT COST $34K

I promised this story in the sub-headline. Here it is.
What broke
Position-sizing had a race condition. When two signals landed in the same 200ms window - one from the SCOUT queue, one from a re-org replay - the sizing function read available_capital twice before either write committed.
Both trades sized as if the full bankroll were available. The desk opened two full-size positions on tokens with correlated narratives. Both dumped in the same 20-minute window.
Damage: -$34,180 over six hours. Recovered in nine days.
Why it took six hours to catch
The bug only fired when two signals overlapped inside 200ms. That happened on average once every 4 days. When it fired, it looked like a normal drawdown, not a bug - two bad trades in a row is not statistically surprising on this desk.
I only caught it because the trade log showed both entries at exactly the same computed size, down to the wei. Coincidence at that precision is impossible.
The fix
Redis-locked position sizing. available_capital reads and the corresponding position write happen inside a single transaction. Latency cost: ~40ms per trade. Race condition eliminated.
The lesson
The desk was built to handle model failures, market failures, and infra failures. It was not built to handle my failures. The 5-agent pipeline is only as safe as the plumbing between the agents.
Every dollar the desk has made since day 20 has come from paranoid engineering on the boring parts. The AI is the loud half. The other half is transaction logs and idempotency keys.

09 · WHAT'S NEXT

The desk is running. It's tuning itself against new patterns as they appear. Three extensions in the pipeline.
Cross-wallet correlation
Right now each insider is judged independently. In practice, groups of insiders move together. Three separate sniper wallets entering the same token in 90 seconds is a stronger signal than three unrelated ones. Coding a correlation layer now.
Sentiment ingest
CONTEXT already reads token socials. The next version subscribes to a filtered stream of narrative-tagged Crypto Twitter and treats a sudden sentiment inflection as a HISTORIAN-level input.
Exit-side agent
Current exit rules are simple: trail stops, 2x trims, dead-time closes. The next version has a sixth agent - REAPER - that reviews every open position on every pulse and adjusts individually. Early tests suggest another 8-12% on average P&L per position.
All three should ship in the next month.
