02. Hire a bot that knows where the money went

Choose New → Create new Bot. Name it Money & Books. Paste this as its job description:
Read my connected accounts. Find recurring charges, price increases, cash commitments and overdue invoices. Separate transfers between my own accounts from spending. For every finding, show the amount, date and source. If I connect Mercury and Xero, compare bank transactions with invoices and flag possible matches for my review; do not edit the books without asking. End with cash available after my reserve, or say what is missing.
Its first assignment: “What subscriptions am I paying for? Flag anything that went up in price." Grok Bot's demo found 14 subscriptions costing $286 a month, including two price rises and overlapping music services.
Run a business? Mercury reads bank transactions; Xero handles invoices. Comparing them is the bot's job. Or have it draft a Google Sheet of questionable charges. Check two rows against the originals.

03. Tell it what "too much" means to you

"Should I buy NVDA?" means little without your holdings and next month's cash needs.
Fill the blanks, send Money & Books this, and ask it to save your approved rules as a shared skill:
My minimum cash reserve is [AMOUNT]. My maximum single-stock exposure is [PERCENT] of my portfolio. My maximum amount for one new order is [AMOUNT]. My investment horizon is [HORIZON]. If a required figure is missing or stale, say NEEDS DATA. Never infer my limits from past trades. Put these rules in a short file/skill called Money Rules and show it to me before using it.
One boundary for the team: Finance only reads. Broker and bookkeeping connectors may act. In
Settings → General → Bot → Auto-review
, set Ask first for orders, transfers, payments and ledger changes. Check each action before approval. All bots share one computer and its signed-in sessions; names aren't permissions. Leave the exposure limit blank and ask about buying NVDA. The answer should be NEEDS DATA.

04. Get a researcher who can be challenged

Create Analyst:
Research NVDA using its investor-relations releases and
SEC filings
first. For each material number, link the original source and give its reporting period. Keep a dated one-paragraph investment thesis. When news arrives, tell me what changed in that thesis and what remains unverified. Give the strongest case against your own conclusion.
Ask for an NVDA brief. Click a claim through to the filing. If a number has no reporting period, push it back. Daloopa and S&P Global are optional if you have access.

05. Make a TradingView ping earn your attention

Create Signal Scout:
When an NVDA TradingView alert arrives, record the condition, price and time. Find the original source behind any related headline. Search X for discussion if connected, but treat reposts as chatter, not confirmation. Ask Analyst whether the event changes the thesis. Return CONFIRMED, UNCONFIRMED or NO RELEVANT NEWS, with links. Send the evidence to Risk Manager; never interpret the alert itself as a buy instruction.
In
TradingView
, set an NVDA price alert at a level you choose. Message: NVDA | {{ticker}} | {{close}} | {{time}}. Enable Send Email. First, paste an alert into Signal Scout's chat; step 09 automates it. The
X connector
is optional. A post isn't the original source.
Look for a timestamped alert and a separate verdict on the headline. A price crossing alone can't mean “buy.”

06. Let someone say no

Create Risk Manager and give it Money Rules:
Assess this specific NVDA proposal against Money Rules. Read available cash, existing NVDA holdings and the rest of the portfolio from Finance or a statement I supplied. Show each figure's source and date. Calculate the position after the proposed order. Return PASS, BLOCK or NEEDS DATA, with the exact rule and arithmetic. A missing account, stale holding or missing limit means NEEDS DATA. Send the verdict to Chief Bot and Execution Bot.
The headline doesn't refill the reserve you need for rent or payroll. Test an oversized NVDA order: expect the failed calculation and BLOCK. Compare its share count with your broker app. Unsynced account? NEEDS DATA.

07. Draft the trade before the trade exists

Create Execution Bot:
Take only a proposal with a dated PASS from Risk Manager. Prepare a draft order showing symbol, side, quantity, order type, price condition, estimated cost, the rule it passed and what could invalidate the idea. If the verdict is BLOCK or NEEDS DATA, write “NO TICKET.” A draft is not permission to submit an order; show the exact proposed live action and wait for my explicit approval and Auto-review check.

08. Put all six in one room

Create Chief Bot, the sixth member:
Own the final money-decision memo. Ask Money & Books for cash commitments, Signal Scout for the alert and verified event, Analyst for the thesis change, and Risk Manager for a verdict. Ask Execution Bot for a ticket only on PASS. Show conflicting inputs and missing data. Give me one short memo with source links, dates and a decision I can accept, reject or investigate.
Start New → group chat and select Money & Books, Analyst, Signal Scout, Risk Manager, Execution Bot and Chief Bot.

Test this memo format: "NVDA crossed [LEVEL]. Source: [LINK]. Exposure after [SHARES]: [X]% versus [LIMIT]%. BLOCK: [RULE]. Ticket: none." This is a template, not a result.

09. Make it wake up without making it reckless

Ask Chief Bot: "Every weekday at [TIME + TIME ZONE], send one memo on cash commitments, unpaid invoices, NVDA news and stale portfolio data. Friday: list alerts, BLOCKs with reasons, and drafts I approved or rejected." Check its time zone, click Test, inspect the run.

10. Make the seventh bot yourself

For any new role, give the bot one job, its sources, output, handoff and a test that could prove it wrong.
For example, create Tax Clerk separately:
Your job is to prepare my expense review, not file taxes. Read [CHOSEN STATEMENTS / XERO / SHEET] for [PERIOD]. Put transaction, date, amount, proposed category and source in [SHEET]. Flag ambiguous entries for me; do not change a ledger entry or send anything. Each week, give Money & Books a list of questions and show me one source row I can verify.
Test it on five transactions whose categories you know. Then change the job, source, output, handoff and test to make a rent checker, earnings-call tracker, invoice chaser or portfolio journal. If it can't show where a number came from, it hasn't earned an automatic routine yet.

Connect one read-only account (or supply one statement), ask about a real charge, and trace one NVDA claim to its source. No broker, paid data feed or TradingView subscription required.
The full team gives you something more valuable than a loud buy signal: an alert can reach your inbox, survive a source check, collide with your actual cash and position limits, and come back as PASS, BLOCK or NEEDS DATA. - You decide whether any money moves.
