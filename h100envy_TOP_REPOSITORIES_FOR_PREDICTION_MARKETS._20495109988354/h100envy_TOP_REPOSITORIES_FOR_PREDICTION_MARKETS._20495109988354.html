Niche categories: sports, weather, elections

These categories are characterized by specific data sources and models. Sports requires ESPN/sportsbook integration. Weather - NWP models (NBM, GFS, ECMWF). Elections - polling data, demographics, historical bases. Generic copy-trading bots lose to specialized ones here.

Election forecasting - methodology

Unlike sports and weather, standalone ML models are rarely built for elections - the market is so information-rich that edge comes from:
1. Cross-platform mispricing (Polymarket vs Kalshi vs PredictIt) - use `pmxt-dev/pmxt` or `CarlosIbCu/polymarket-kalshi-btc-arbitrage-bot` as a template
2. State-level vs national markets - arbitrage between linked contracts via `0xalberto/polymarket-arbitrage-bot`
3. News-driven AI agents - `Polymarket/agents` or `OctagonAI/kalshi-trading-bot-cli` filtered by category=politics
